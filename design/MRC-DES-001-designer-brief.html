Moracat designer brief

Moracat gives every cat in Saudi Arabia a lifelong ID: a numbered card with a QR code, a health record that travels to any clinic, and a page in a community of cat families. The product is live and works. These are the visual pieces that a graphic designer, a 3D artist and a photographer will do better than code.

For graphic designer · 3D artist · photographer Language Arabic first (RTL), English second Site moracat.co
00

Brand basics

The direction is called «السجل» (the register): every artifact Moracat issues looks like a page from the cat's personal archive. An ID band names the document, ruled rows hold the facts, and a small copper seal marks what Moracat vouches for. The public marketing pages are warmer and playful: rounded shapes, sparkles, stickers and soft 3D objects.

Three rules that override everything else. The cat is always the hero of the frame. Moracat is a private company: never use the words official, government, national or census of Saudi Arabia, and never imitate a state ID. Arabic is the main language, so design every piece in Arabic first and mirror it for English.

Live content in every template

  • Cat photo: square, shown at about 900 × 900 px.
  • Cat name: 2–24 characters, Arabic or Latin.
  • One or two short lines: district, date, or "missing since…".
  • Serial in mono: MRC-XXXX-XXXX.
  • QR code: at least 240 × 240 px, on white, with a quiet zone.
  • The address moracat.co.
Lost and Found are distress posters. Use alert red, no 3D objects, no cute stickers and no jokes. They must be readable as a small WhatsApp thumbnail: the photo, the word "lost" and the QR come first. The owner's phone number never appears; the QR leads finders to a private message form.

RuleRequirement
File namesLowercase with hyphens: moracat-{asset}-{variant}[-ar|-en][@2x].{ext}
Screen coloursRGB IEC 61966-2.1, embedded in every PNG and JPEG
Print colourCMYK (FOGRA39 unless the print shop asks otherwise), 3 mm bleed (2 mm for the card), 300 dpi images, text outlined, PDF/X-4
VectorsSVG optimised and editable: real paths, no embedded raster, no hidden layers
SourcesFigma (preferred) with named components; editable .ai for print; Blender scenes for 3D
FontsUse the faces in Brand basics. Lyon Arabic Display is licensed to Moracat: ask us for a working copy, check the license covers your use in comps, and outline it in all exports.
RightsFull transfer of copyright, or an exclusive, perpetual, worldwide licence including the right to modify. List any third-party element (texture, font, stock) and its licence.
Review roundsConcepts first (one or two directions per P0 item), then one refinement round, then final files
Order of workP0 (logo, icons, 3D) first; P1 (card, stickers, posters, wallet, photos) next; P2 (link previews, certificate, email, tag) last

Before you send anything, check

  • Designed in Arabic first and mirrored for English, with no letter-spacing on Arabic.
  • No "official", "government" or "national" wording and no state-like emblems.
  • No 3D objects or cute stickers on lost or found pieces.
  • Every size and format in this brief matches the file you are sending.
  • Text is at least 4.5:1 against its background, and QR codes sit on white with a quiet zone.
