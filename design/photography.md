# Moracat — photography brief (AD 2.1)

_2026-10-01 · for the founder's first shoot and for anyone choosing member photos_

**The cat is the hero.** Photography is how a member's cat, and not a stock
cat, becomes the face of Moracat. Two directions, used for different jobs.

## 1 · The Official Portrait («صورة الهوية»)
The photo that sits on a Cat ID, the Wallet pass, the poster and the keepsake.
- Eye level with the cat, square crop, the head in the upper third, eyes sharp.
- A plain warm ground: paper, linen, a painted wall (`#FAF7F2` family). No props.
- Soft window light from one side. No flash and no ring-light catchlights.
- It must still read at 96px. One cat per photo.
- Never retouch markings. They are how a finder recognises the cat.

**In-product guidance** (photo upload and "complete the file"): «صوّره في ضوء
النهار، على مستوى عينه، وخلفية سادة» / "Daylight, eye level, a plain background."

## 2 · Majlis Level («مستوى المجلس»)
Editorial photography for the homepage, the about page and social posts. The cat
in a real Saudi home.
- The camera at floor or majlis-cushion height, the cat's eye line.
- Real interiors: sadu cushions, a window with a mashrabiya shadow, a
  coffee tray at the edge of the frame. Lived-in, not styled.
- People appear only as hands, laps and feet. Never faces, unless they are
  members who have signed a release.
- Natural colour: warm, a little underexposed and calm. Never pastel-graded.

## What we never do
- Stock cats on product surfaces. A member's own cat is always better than a
  "perfect" stranger.
- Photos of sick, injured or distressed animals in marketing.
- Text over a cat's face, or crops that cut the ears.
- AI-generated cats presented as members.

## Using member photos
The homepage "cats of Moracat" strip draws **only** from cats that are already
published to the community (opt-out model, with a required photo). It honours
each member's privacy setting at render time. Any campaign beyond the site
(ads, print) needs a separate, explicit release from the member.

## Shot list for the first shoot (about 12 cats, one day)
1. Six Official Portraits, mixed breeds, including at least two baladi (local street) cats.
2. Three Majlis Level scenes: morning window, evening family majlis, a kitten
   on a cushion.
3. A collar-tag close-up on a real cat, for the identity chapter (until the 3D
   collar is commissioned).
4. A vet-visit moment: a carrier on a clinic counter, hands only (for vets).
5. A reunion: a cat back in a member's arms, faces out of frame.
