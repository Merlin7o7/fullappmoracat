# MRC-FIN-002 — Pricing Model v2: Value-First Rebuild

**Date:** 2026-07-23 · **Status:** Committed strategy (founder decision this session) — supersedes the plan structure in `seed-catalog.ts` and the scenario tables in MRC-FIN-001 §3
**Philosophy:** value-first, transparent, easy to understand. Essentials contain only necessities. Every tier builds on the previous. The number of cats is the value metric.
**Evidence base:** MRC-FIN-001 market-price sweep (5 KSA retailers, 2026-07-22) + two research reports (pricing science; KSA spending & feline consumption, 2026-07-23). ✅ = verified/sourced · ⚠️ = estimate.

Amazon.sa S&S = 5–15%. **Nobody in KSA ships an honest full month with a kitten tier and per-cat scaling. That is the position this model takes.**

Strictly additive: every tier contains everything below it. Names bilingual; prices charm-on-hundreds (R10).

It is the trust anchor and the decoy-adjacent entry (R1/R5): the value jump to Complete is designed to be unmissable.

## 4. Kitten plan — قطتي الصغيرة — **199 SAR/mo** (acquisition tier, R9)

Stage-aware (3–8 months), not a shrunken adult box — kitten intake at 4–8mo actually *exceeds* a small adult's ✅:
Josera Kitten 2kg (46) · 15× kitten wet — Petite Kitten pouches + Kitten Mousse cans mix (~53) · Kit Cat Classic Clump 10L (19) → COGS **118** (neg. 106.2) · DIY ~192 → **parity**, and −9% vs adult Essentials ("meaningfully cheaper" ✓).

GM **46.6% negotiated** ✅ · contribution (neg): 33.7 = 16.9%; VAT era: **7.8 = 4.4% — deliberately near-break-even.** This is priced as *paid acquisition*: no KSA competitor has a kitten tier, the kitten owner is the 15-year relationship, and Chewy's $20–30 first-order subsidies are the precedent. The CAC ledger, not the box P&L, is where this tier reports.

- **Kitten Welcome gift (first box, gated on a 3-month term — R7 gift logic):** toy + wipes + bilingual kitten guide + growth-chart card for the Cat ID profile (~25 COGS, ~65 perceived).
- **Auto-graduation:** the feeding engine knows the birthday — at ~9 months the plan proposes Essentials/Complete with a ceremony moment ("she's grown 🐾"). Stage transition is a product feature, priced in, never a silent price rise.

## 5. Multi-cat households (up to 6 cats) — the household-box model

**Architecture:** one household subscription, one bigger shared box (R5 — the KitNipBox pattern): the *care layer* (wipes, calming, supplements) is shared; each cat adds a **consumables module** (their food + their litter share — each module tied to that cat's Cat ID, contents computed by the feeding engine). Consumable discounts stay shallow (PrettyLitter −8% floor ✅); the visible per-cat price drop comes from paying shipping/packaging/care-layer once. Curve flattens at cat 4 (R6); cats 5–6 price flat at the marginal rate. At 2–3 cats litter switches to Kit Cat MultiCat 20kg formula (39 vs 2×19 — same cost, better product ✅honest).

**Module prices:** Essentials +180/cat · Complete +280/cat · Signature +400/cat · Kitten module +180 (kitten SKUs, addable to any household).

Weight: 2 cats ≈ 26kg (one large parcel ⚠️ verify), ≥3 cats ships as 2+ parcels (modeled at +30/parcel).

## 6. Terms, renewal & gifts

- **Default: monthly auto-renew** (Moyasar tokenized mada/Apple Pay — the MRC-LAUNCH-001 integration; pre-charge notice at T-7). Tamara remains a checkout *option* for prepaid terms.
- **Prepay:** 3-mo = base · **6-mo −5% + free add-on pack each quarter** (toy+treat, COGS ~15, perceived ~45) · **12-mo −8% + gifts + founding price-lock**. Research norm is −15/−25% (R7), but at 13–16% VAT-era contribution, −18% is arithmetic suicide (a 12-mo Complete at −18% has *negative* VAT-era contribution). Gifts bridge the credibility gap at 1/3 the margin cost. **Phase 2 (COGS −20% via §8): move to −10/−18%.**
- **Dunning (R8):** failed-renewal grace week + mada retry + WhatsApp nudge before any lapse — ~40% of churn is involuntary; this is the cheapest LTV lever we own.

**Today-COGS (no negotiation) check ⚠️:** Complete GM falls to 39.1% (below R4) and VAT-era contribution to ~21 SAR. **The −10% distributor negotiation is a gate for launch at these prices** — it is realistic (MRC-FIN-001 §4: we currently pay ~ordinary retailer wholesale; the direct-import quote is the leverage), but if it fails, Complete must go to 349, not eat the margin.

### 7.2 VAT posture — the threshold math kills the old assumption
Average revenue/household ≈ **350 SAR/mo** (mix ⚠️: 20% Essentials / 55% Complete / 15% Signature / 10% Kitten, ×1.3 avg cats). Mandatory VAT registration at SAR 375,000/yr ≈ **90 households**. Ninety. There is no meaningful pre-VAT era — **this model is built on VAT-registered numbers from day one**, and the 0%-VAT column above is only the (short) runway bonus. Register voluntarily now (input-VAT recovery on packaging/freight/COGS also depends on it).

### 7.3 LTV (VAT-era, auto-renew)
Complete, 7% monthly churn (R8) → 14.3-mo tenure → **LTV ≈ 558**; blended with prepay mix (annual churns at ⅓) → ~650. Multi-cat households (avg 1.3 cats) → **~730 blended**. LTV:CAC ≥ 3 ⇒ **CAC ceiling ≈ 210–240** — paid ads must clear this or stay census/referral-led. (Old model, Tamara-only manual renewal: LTV ~386 — this rebuild ≈ **1.9× LTV**.)

### 7.4 Break-even & milestone P&L (VAT-era, negotiated COGS ⚠️)
Contribution/household ≈ **46/mo** (mix-blended incl. modules). Replacement marketing = 7% churn × CAC 180.

| Households | Revenue/mo | Contribution | Opex | Repl. CAC | **Net/mo** |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 100 | 35,000 | 4,600 | 14,000 (founder) | 1,260 | **−10,660** |
| 250 | 87,500 | 11,500 | 16,000 | 3,150 | **−7,650** |
| 500 | 175,000 | 23,000 | 22,000 (+packer) | 6,300 | **−5,300** |
| **~750** | 262,500 | 34,500 | 24,000 | 9,450 | **≈ +1,050 — break-even** |
| 1,000 | 350,000 | 46,000 | 30,000 (lean) | 12,600 | **+3,400** |
| 2,500 (Phase-2 COGS −20% → ~64/hh) | 875,000 | 160,000 | 80,000 | 31,500 | **+48,500** |
| 5,000 (Phase-2) | 1,750,000 | 320,000 | 150,000 | 63,000 | **+107,000** |

**Honest reading:** Phase 1 (distributor, −10%) reaches founder-lean profitability at **~750 households** (vs 945 subs in the dead model — and these are *households*, ~975 cats) but cannot fund a real team. **Phase 2 sourcing (−20% via the MRC-FIN-001 litter container + negotiation escalation at ~500 households) is what makes 1,000+ scale-profitable.** Pricing alone was never going to fix this; the model now says so out loud.

### 7.5 Value-ledger rule (replaces the broken invariant)
`minMemberSaving` is redefined **against the market benchmark basket** (per-SKU `marketPrice` from the quarterly 5-store sweep, MRC-FIN-001 dataset v1): Essentials ≥ −10% (parity band, no savings claim) · Complete ≥ +3% genuine saving · Signature ≥ +8%. The box page prints the ledger item-by-item at market prices — auditable by any member with a Petzone tab open (R006).
