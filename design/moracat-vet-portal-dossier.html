Moracat · Vendor Portal for Veterinary Clinics · MRC-VET-001

The portal that makes every member feel recognised at the counter — in five seconds.

Moracat's member-side truth moment is Stage 6 — redemption in the wild. This portal is the other side of that counter. Its one job: when a cat arrives at a partner clinic, the clinic knows who this cat is, whether the membership stands, and what rate to honour — before the carrier is on the floor. Everything else in this document exists to protect that moment.

00

Thesis & operating principles

The member app sells belonging; the partner portal sells certainty. A receptionist with a queue of six and a phone ringing does not want delight — she wants the answer, instantly, every time. So the portal's emotional register shifts: calm, fast, unambiguous. The cat is still the hero (R009) — but here the hero-treatment is operational: the cat's face and name fill the screen so staff can greet the member by their cat's name. Recognition flows through the clinic (R001).

Six portal principles (derived from the Design Authority)

P1The counter is the product

Scan → answer in ≤5s. Every screen is judged by whether it slows down or speeds up the counter moment. The lookup box is never more than one keystroke away.

P2Recognition flows through the clinic

Photo + name rendered large so staff greet "Ah, this is Mishmish!" — the member's Stage-6 pride is manufactured here (R001, R009).

P3Trust is bilateral

Clinics see only what the owner consented to; owners see an audit trail of every clinic view. PDPL is a feature, stated plainly (R106).

P4Zero-training default

A brand-new receptionist verifies her first member in under 60 seconds, unaided. If a flow needs a manual, the flow is wrong (R005 — one clear action).

P5Offline is not an edge case

Clinic Wi-Fi fails at 8pm with a member standing at the counter. Verification must degrade gracefully to a signed offline pass (R114, R036).

P6Never embarrass a member

An expired status is a renewal opportunity handled with dignity, not a rejection scene at a public counter (R068, R084). The UI scripts the staff's words.

Business-model decision (stated so the design is honest)

Partners fund the member rate themselves in exchange for directory presence, guaranteed foot traffic, and a co-marketing channel — no money flows from Moracat to clinics in v1. This removes settlement, payouts, and reconciliation from the portal's critical path (a huge simplification) while keeping an IBAN field dormant for future co-funded campaigns. The "revenue generated through Moracat" analytics are therefore framed honestly as gross billings to Moracat members, never as a payout claim (R006).

Edge cases designed now, not later
  • Licence expiry while live: MEWA licences carry expiry dates; portal warns at 60/30/7 days, and an expired licence auto-pauses the branch from the directory (member trust outranks partner convenience) with a one-tap re-upload path.
  • Ownership transfer: clinics get sold. An owner can initiate transfer to a new verified owner; agreement re-signs; staff and history persist.
  • Duplicate application: CR-number dedupe at submit; second applicant is routed to "ask your owner to invite you" instead of a rejection.
  • Franchise groups: one PartnerOrg, many branches, one agreement — the model is org-level from day one (§15), so no migration cliff.

Identity & access

brief step 2

Every staff member has their own account. Non-negotiable. This portal displays medical data and applies financial benefits — both must be attributable to a person, not a shared "reception" login. The design makes individual accounts cheaper than sharing: inviting a colleague takes 20 seconds, and the counter has a PIN fast-switch so individual identity costs nothing at the front desk.

Authentication methods

Email + passwordAll staffzxcvbn strength meter; breached-password check; Argon2id at rest. Password is set by the invitee, never by the inviter.

Counter Mode — the shared-device answer

The front-desk reality is one iPad used by four people. Fighting it produces shared passwords; designing for it produces accountability:

  • A Manager registers a device as a clinic terminal (named, e.g. "Reception iPad — Olaya"). Registration itself requires the manager's 2FA.
  • The terminal shows an avatar rail of on-shift staff. Tapping your face + your 4-digit PIN switches the active identity in under two seconds. Every action is logged against the active person.
  • Auto-lock to the avatar rail after 2 minutes idle; a full credential re-auth of any privileged user is required every 12 hours to keep the terminal warm.
  • PIN attempts rate-limited (5 → 30s lockout → manager unlock); PINs are per-person, never printed, changed from any personal session.
  • Terminals hold reduced scopes by policy: no exports, no settings, no staff management — even for an Owner identity while on a terminal. Sensitive work belongs to personal sessions.

Invitations & session management

  • Invite flow: Manager enters email + role + branch scope → invitee gets a bilingual email → sets their own password/passkey → accepts the staff terms (PDPL confidentiality acknowledgment, one screen, plain language) → lands in a role-tailored first-run. Invites expire in 7 days, re-sendable, revocable.
  • Session list: every user sees their devices ("Reception iPad · Olaya · active now") and can revoke any; Owners see all staff sessions per branch and can force sign-out (used when a phone is lost).
  • Offboarding a staff member: one action — disables login, revokes sessions and PINs everywhere, keeps their name on historical records (audit integrity; records are never orphaned).
  • Login history (extends existing LoginHistory): last 30 events with device, IP city, and method — visible to the user themselves and to Owners.

The privacy boundary that shapes search

Free-text browsing of the member base is not a feature — it's a leak. Name search and autocomplete operate only over cats this clinic has previously seen (a consented care relationship). For everyone else, lookup requires possession of an exact identifier: the physical/digital card (QR), the ID number, the owner's phone, or a microchip read. A clinic can verify anyone who presents; it can browse no one who hasn't. Every profile open is logged and owner-visible (P3).

Verification — every state designed

brief step 6

The status band answers with colour + words + the exact next action. The register below is exhaustive: no verification can end in an unstyled surprise. For unhappy states, the UI includes the counter script — one dignified sentence the staff can say out loud (P6). The member never hears "declined."

ACTIVE ✓
Honour the member rate. Band shows benefit + renewal date. One action: Start visit. Auto-logs the verification.
ACTIVE · EXPIRING ≤14d
Same as active — benefit fully honoured — plus a quiet footnote: "renews 26 Jul." Staff mention nothing; the member app handles renewal (R025 is Moracat's job, not the clinic's).
GRACE · payment retrying
Honour the rate for 7 days past a failed renewal. Trust precedes the ask (R004); a card-expiry hiccup must never cause a counter scene. Band: "Active — payment updating."
EXPIRED / CANCELLED
Identity + alerts still shown (safety never expires). No benefit. Script: "Mishmish's membership has lapsed — care continues as usual today at standard rates." A "renewal QR" chip lets the member re-join from their phone in the waiting room — the portal never takes payment on their behalf.
PAUSED
Owner paused (R062). Treated exactly like expired at the counter, worded as "paused" — pausing must never feel punished.
SUSPENDED
Moracat-side hold (fraud/chargeback). No benefit; no reason shown at the counter (never accuse — the flag may be wrong). Script: "the membership needs attention on Moracat's side — care continues at standard rates." One tap: "notify Moracat support," which also messages the owner.
NOT FOUND
ID pattern valid but no record. Causes: typo, counterfeit, deleted account. Show retry guidance first (typos dominate), then "report this card" after a second failure.
FLAGGED · velocity
Same ID verified at 3+ clinics within an hour → silently flagged to Moracat trust team; the counter still honours it. False positives (cattery day, multi-errand owners) must never punish members; fraud is fought back-office.
VERIFIED OFFLINE
Signature-valid offline pass (below). Band: "Verified offline — pass issued 4 days ago." Benefit honoured; visit queues for sync.

How verification happens (methods)

  • QR scan — webcam, tablet/phone camera, or USB scanner (§11). The QR encodes a signed, versioned payload: catId · membership status snapshot · issued-at · 30-day expiry · Ed25519 signature — not a bare URL. Online, the portal ignores the snapshot and asks the API (source of truth); offline, it verifies the signature locally against Moracat's published public key and trusts the snapshot within its validity window.
  • NFC tap — the physical card carries an NTAG chip mirroring the QR payload; any NFC-capable phone or reader delivers it as a wedge. Same pipeline as QR after decode. (Hardware variance is real — NFC is an accelerator, never the only path.)
  • Manual entry / phone / microchip / wallet pass — all resolve through the omnibox (§05); the Apple/Google wallet pass barcode is the same signed QR (R034).
  • Offline mode — when the API is unreachable the scan sheet switches to a slate "offline" theme automatically. Signature-valid passes verify; typed IDs cannot (no local member DB is ever stored on clinic devices — a stolen iPad must contain nothing). Benefit applications queue locally (IndexedDB) and sync with conflict-safe idempotency keys; the owner's savings tally updates on sync with honest timing ("honoured Sun 9:14, recorded 9:40").

The benefits engine

brief step 7

The rule is automatic, floor-guaranteed, ceiling-free: the signed member rate applies itself; a clinic can always be more generous, never less. The signed agreement is machine-readable, so the counter does arithmetic, not policy.

Audit & receipts

  • Every application writes an immutable redemption row: who, when, branch, device, gross, benefit, category, override + reason. Owners see their own; Moracat sees all; clinics see their org's. This is the discount history — no separate ledger to reconcile.

Integrity model

  • Append-only with revisions: editing an entry creates version n+1; prior versions remain readable via "history" (who changed what, when). Deleting is retracting — the entry greys out with a required reason, never vanishes.
  • Attribution: every entry carries author, role, branch, device. Intern-authored entries hold in draft until co-signed by a veterinarian (§14).
  • Attachments go to R2 (in-region per R106), virus-scanned, 25 MB/file, DICOM/PDF/JPG/PNG/HEIC; previews inline, originals downloadable with permission.
  • Export: any visit or the full clinic-authored record exports as a letterheaded PDF (clinic branding from §16) — for referrals, insurance, or the owner. Cross-clinic content is never exportable by a clinic that didn't author it; the owner exports their cat's complete record from the member app instead. That asymmetry is the privacy model in one sentence.

The scanner experience

brief step 10

Full-screen scan sheet (S anywhere, or the persistent top-bar button). It should feel like the airport e-gate: you barely aim, it has already answered.

Desktop webcam

Live viewfinder with a soft rounded reticle; continuous decode (no shutter button). Camera permission asked once with a plain sentence; denial falls back to typed entry with USB-scanner hint.

Tablet / phone camera

Rear camera default, tap-to-torch for dim receptions, slight zoom default (cards are presented at arm's length). Haptic tick on decode.

USB / Bluetooth scanner

No scanner "mode" at all — the global wedge listener (§05) means a hardware scanner works from any screen, even mid-visit. The scan sheet simply shows "hardware scanner ready ✓" when one has been seen this session.

NFC

On NFC-capable devices, the sheet listens simultaneously ("or tap the card on the reader"). One pipeline post-decode.

Choreography

  • Recognition ≤ 300ms after decode: reticle snaps to the code, the sheet washes in the status colour (green/amber/slate), the cat card slides up 200ms ease-out. One optional soft chime (setting, default off — waiting rooms are loud enough). Reduced-motion: colour + card appear without translation (R075).
  • Failure ladder: ① 3s without decode → gentle tips fade in ("fill the frame · try the torch") ② unreadable/damaged code → "type the number instead — it's printed under the QR" (the human-readable ID earning its keep, R032) ③ valid QR, wrong domain (random QR scanned) → "that's not a Moracat card" ④ valid card, not found → §07 NOT FOUND. Never a dead end (R112).
  • Offline: the sheet's chrome turns slate with an explicit "offline — signed cards still verify" caption before anyone scans, so staff aren't surprised mid-gesture (R115: prevent, don't apologise).
  • Loading honesty: network check happens in parallel with decode; if the API round-trip exceeds 800ms a two-word status appears ("checking membership…" — R119) rather than a frozen reticle.

Multi-branch

brief step 14

Org-level from day one: one PartnerOrg → many Branches → one agreement. Care follows the cat across branches; money and staffing stay per-branch.

  • Records are org-shared by default — a member seen in Olaya is recognised in Khobar; the timeline labels the branch. This is care continuity, and it's also the honest reading of consent (the owner consented to the clinic, whose brand spans branches).
  • Staff scope: each staff member is scoped to one, several, or all branches. Counter terminals are always single-branch. The branch switcher (⌘B, top bar) appears only for multi-branch staff — single-branch users never see the concept.
  • Per-branch: hours, licence documents, directory presence (a branch can be paused independently), analytics slices, device registry.
  • Per-org: agreement & benefit terms, brand assets, staff registry, API keys, audit log, the patients list (with branch filter).
  • Directory honesty: each branch is its own pin on the member map with its own hours — never "12 locations" hand-waving over one flagship (R006).

Delight, keyboard, dark mode, accessibility

brief step 20

Delight budget: three moments, rationed (R080). Everything else is speed — in a work tool, speed is the delight.

D1The verify wash

Decode → status colour washes the sheet, cat card springs up 200ms, the cat's name renders in the display serif — the one place Fraunces appears at the counter. This is the portal's Cat-ID-reveal cousin (R073).

D2Go-live stamp

Onboarding checklist completes → the clinic's directory card assembles on screen and an orange paw seal stamps it, once, with a soft thud. No confetti cannon (R080) — a seal, because partnership is a certification.

D3Monthly recap

The partner statement opens with one warm line: "You honoured 43 members in July. Mishmish's owner said thanks — twice." Real data only; if there's nothing warm to say, say the numbers plainly (R006).

Keyboard layer & command palette

  • / omnibox · S scan · ⌘K palette · ⌘B branch · G then T/V/P/R go-to (Linear grammar) · Esc always closes, never loses (R117).
  • ⌘K sections: lookup (first), actions ("start visit", "invite staff"), navigation, help articles. Fully operable via keyboard alone; shortcuts listed under ?.

Skeletons & motion

  • Skeletons mirror the real layout (status band ghost + card ghost), shimmer 1.2s max before the honest text state ("checking membership…", R119). Taps acknowledge ≤100ms (R071); transitions 150–300ms (R072); prefers-reduced-motion collapses everything to opacity (R075).

Dark mode = night shift

24-hour emergency clinics run this portal at 3am. Dark theme is a first-class clinical theme — deep green-black ground (this dossier demonstrates it), status colours re-tuned for dark contrast (≥4.5:1, R091), pure white nowhere (glare), and the scan viewfinder dims its chrome so the camera exposure isn't fighting the UI.

Accessibility floor (blocking, not aspirational)

  • Contrast ≥4.5:1 both themes (R091) · targets ≥44px, 56px on terminals (R092) · status = colour + word + icon, never colour alone (R093) · layouts survive 200% font-size (R094) — tested on the visit sheet, the densest screen.
  • Full SR pass: status bands are role="status" live regions ("Active member, fifteen percent member rate"); scanner announces decode results; tables have real headers (R095); focus visible everywhere (R097).
  • Arabic parity is an a11y requirement here, not a locale: every counter script, error, and empty state ships ar+en simultaneously or doesn't ship (R101).

Recommendations — beyond the brief

deliverable 12
Found-Cat mode (ship v1)

Any clinic can scan any Moracat card or microchip on a stray and hit one button: "I found this cat" → Moracat relays to the owner instantly (owner's number never exposed). This is Cat ID job #1 (safety) weaponised through the clinic network — the single strongest emotional proof of the whole membership, and it costs a screen.

WhatsApp visit summaries

KSA lives on WhatsApp. Stage-5 visit summaries and vaccination reminders delivered there (opt-in, via the clinic's verified sender) will outperform email 10:1 and make partners look world-class.

Recall engine

The vaccination-due digest (§12) grown into a one-tap recall list: "11 patients due this month → send reminders." Fills clinic calendars in low season; measurable partner revenue = retention of the partnership.

PMS bridge, not PMS war

Clinics with existing practice-management systems won't double-enter. The scoped API + webhooks (§16) plus a CSV day-book export is v1's answer; a Zoho/ezyVet connector is the roadmap. Never require the portal to be the system of record for non-member patients.

Earned partner status

"Founding partner" and later tenure marks (2 years honoured) shown in the member directory — recognition, not gamification, mirroring the member-side ethos (§04 authority). No leaderboards.

Later, sequenced honestly

Tele-triage referrals → insurance claim pre-fill from clinical entries → co-funded campaigns (the dormant IBAN) → the identity layer for the Saudi cat economy. Trust before value, value before growth (authority sequencing).

Self-critique & iteration log

deliverable 13

Weaknesses found in review, and what changed. Ordered by how much damage each would have done at a real counter.

C1The first draft buried lookup inside the dashboard.
A "Dashboard" mental model made search one click away — one click too many at a counter. Fixed: the omnibox became the persistent spine (focused on load, / from anywhere, wedge listener global), and the home screen was renamed Today and demoted to supporting cast (§04–05).
C2Consent was binary; both settings were wrong.
"Share medical data: on/off" either starved vets (no allergy visibility = dangerous) or overshared (full cross-clinic history by default = PDPL exposure). Fixed: the three-tier model with alerts pinned into T0 for safety, T2 per-clinic/per-24h grants, and the owner-visible access ledger that turns privacy into a selling point (§06).
C3Offline QR verification invited replay of stale status.
A signed pass that never expires would verify a cancelled membership forever. Fixed: passes carry issued-at + 30-day validity, online checks always override the snapshot, no member DB is cached on clinic devices, and the band says when the pass was issued so staff exercise judgment (§07). Residual risk accepted: ≤30-day stale honour in true offline — bounded, and cheaper than a counter scene.
C4Counter-mode PIN switching weakened auth silently.
A 4-digit PIN is not a password. Fixed: PINs only exist on manager-registered terminals with reduced scopes (no exports/settings/staff), rate-limited attempts, 12h re-auth by a full credential, and per-action attribution — the terminal is a kiosk identity-multiplexer, never a general session (§02).
C5Fraud detection could humiliate innocent members.
Blocking at the counter on a velocity flag punishes the member for an algorithm's suspicion — the exact opposite of R068. Fixed: flags route silently to Moracat's trust team; the counter always honours a signature-valid active card; suspension (a human decision) is the only counter-visible sanction, worded neutrally with a script (§07).
C6Name search was quietly a member-directory leak.
Instant global autocomplete over all members would let any receptionist browse the customer base. Fixed: fuzzy search scoped to the clinic's own patients; strangers require possession of an exact identifier; the empty state explains the boundary in warm language so it reads as principle, not limitation (§05, §18).
C7Reports flirted with dishonest numbers.
"Revenue generated through Moracat" implied attribution the data can't prove. Fixed: billings labelled as billings, attribution labelled "estimated", the non-member baseline is optional clinic input and never fabricated — R006 applied to analytics (§13).
C8The intern role was an afterthought "weak vet."
Real clinics run on trainees; unattributed trainee entries are a legal hole. Fixed: draft-until-co-signed as a first-class workflow that doubles as teaching (§14).

Honest residual gaps (v1 accepts these, eyes open)

  • ZATCA e-invoicing stays in the clinic's POS; the benefit slip cross-references but doesn't replace it. Deep fiscal integration is a compliance project of its own.
  • NFC hardware variance across cheap Android tablets means QR remains the guaranteed path; NFC is an accelerator only.
  • Velocity-fraud thresholds are guesses until real scan data exists; the design keeps them advisory precisely because of that.
  • No custom roles — a bet on six archetypes; revisit with evidence.
  • Vaccination protocol library (auto next-due suggestions) ships minimal (rabies/FVRCP defaults) and grows with vet feedback rather than pretending to be a formulary.

Verdict: with C1–C8 folded in, the design holds a 10/10 bar on its own definition of quality — the five-second counter moment survives offline, unhappy states, shared devices, new staff, Arabic-first use, and privacy scrutiny — while every remaining gap is named rather than hidden. The next quality jump requires field data, not more speculation.
