Moracat launch audit

A full UX/UI audit of Moracat before public launch: cat owner and veterinary clinic, desktop and mobile, Arabic first. Read from the code on feat/cat-is-the-hero (HEAD 5cda15f) and checked against production www.moracat.co and the local dev stack.

Method. Production public pages were captured on an iPhone 13 profile and at a 1440 px desktop width, with tap-target, type-size and overflow measurements. The logged-in portal was captured locally with test members owning 0, 1 and 3 cats. Four code audits covered the owner journey, the vet portal (including a client/API contract check), the design system and RTL, and copy, trust and conversion. Every P0 below was confirmed in source by me, not just by an agent. Not covered: real-device iOS behaviour, live payments, and the iOS app.

Part 01Executive verdict
No. I would not launch this today.

The owner side looks better than at any earlier audit. The Cat ID ceremony, the register language, the honest "we sell nothing today" copy and the RTL engineering are genuinely good, and some of it is world-class. But a public launch would expose four things the polish can't cover:

  • The clinic side doesn't work. Every clinical record save is rejected by the API. "Start visit" opens /vet/visits/undefined, empty visits can't be closed, and the Today screen's Emergency button leads to a 404. A clinic that tries Moracat once will not try it twice.
  • Members can't come back. Signup has been passwordless since W8, but /login only accepts a password. A returning owner on a new phone gets an error and no explanation.
  • The product makes privacy promises it doesn't keep. It says «بياناتك محفوظة داخل السعودية» while the API runs in Frankfurt. The privacy policy says profiles are private by default, but they've been public by default since August. It says «رقمك لا يظهر لأحد», but the printed collar card shows the phone number. And the first notification every member receives says «رسمياً». For a product holding health records under PDPL, this is the most dangerous category of defect.
  • On a phone, the first screen sells nothing. An iPhone 13 visitor sees a headline and a paragraph. There is no cat, no card, and the register button is hidden under the cookie banner. The header's register button is removed below sm, and the sticky CTA waits for cookie consent.

What's needed before launch: the four items above, plus waitlist consent on the signup path, which today makes the «نخبرك أول ما نفتح» promise impossible to keep. None of these is a redesign. Together they're about two weeks of focused engineering and copy work (see NOW).

The strategic gap that remains after those fixes: the product still struggles to answer "why would I pay?" There are no partners, no savings, no physical tag, and a price grid for plans nobody can buy. During Community Mode that's acceptable only if the free identity is unmistakably valuable on its own, and the cat (the real cat, in a photo) has to be the hero everywhere. Today the most-seen cat in the logged-in product is a pink plush toy.

Part 02Top 10 problemsranked by damage
1
P0The clinic portal cannot record medicine
Problem
The entry composer sends {clinicalType, …fields} inside payload (components/vet/entry-composer.tsx:496). The API validates every payload with forbidNonWhitelisted (apps/api/src/vet/dto/vet-record.dto.ts), and no DTO declares clinicalType. Field names also differ for 11 of 14 entry types (for example batch/batchNo, drug/medication, respRate/respiratoryRate). Knock-on failures:
• "Start visit" navigates to visit.id, but the API returns {visit, resumed}, so the vet lands on /vet/visits/undefined.
• closeVisit sends {}, and an empty visit needs a reason.
• Prescription status sends {status} instead of {action}.
• Counter-PIN sessions never store their token, so every action is attributed to the signed-in account and the toast reads "Welcome, undefined".
• The Today screen's Emergency button pushes /vet/emergency, which doesn't exist (only [catId] does).
Why it matters
The vet record is the moat (MRC-STRAT-001: "clinic-written Cat ID"). Today no clinic can write one. The PIN bug also breaks a promise printed on the vet login screen: that every action is attributed to a person, not a device.
Who
Every clinic. Indirectly every owner, since there are no vet-verified vaccinations, reminders or certificates.
Fix
One shared DTO contract in packages/core, imported by both sides. Every vet call goes through lib/vet-wire.ts. Add an e2e test per entry type, plus scan → open visit → vaccinate → dispense → close → summary → PIN attribution. Re-point Emergency to /vet/scan?intent=emergency, and make the scan page honour that intent.
Impact
Turns the clinic side from a demo into a product. Nothing else on this list matters to a clinic until this works.
2
P0Members who signed up can't sign back in
Problem
Signup (components/start-flow.tsx) uses /auth/email/start|continue and never creates a password. app/login/page.tsx offers only email + password, or SMS behind NEXT_PUBLIC_SMS_ENABLED, which is off. The API already supports code sign-in for existing accounts; the login page simply doesn't call it. The subtitle «سجّل الدخول لإدارة عضويتك» also refers to a membership nobody has.
Why it matters
Every member who clears their browser, changes phone or lets the session expire is locked out. The only escape is "forgot password", which nothing points them to. If they go back through «أنشئ حساباً», the signup flow signs them into the existing account and auto-issues a duplicate cat.
Who
Every owner who signed up since 2026-10-01 (W8, already on production).
Fix
Make email code the default on /login: «أرسل لي رمز الدخول», reusing start-flow's send/verify. Password becomes a secondary link. Change the subtitle to «ادخل لملف قطك». Before auto-issuing on the signup path, check for an existing cat with the same name.
Impact
Retention goes from structurally broken to normal. Probably the cheapest high-impact fix on this list.
3
P0Privacy and legitimacy claims that aren't true
Problem
  • «بياناتك محفوظة داخل السعودية» / «نحفظها داخل السعودية» (lib/i18n.ts:95,109). The API runs in region: frankfurt (render.yaml:10), with storage on R2 and email on Resend.
  • The privacy policy says «ملف قطك خاص افتراضياً» (lib/legal.ts:76, dated 2026-07-08). Since 2026-08-14 cats are public by default, and the signup path forces sharePublicly: true without ever showing the people-in-photo attestation.
  • Signup promises «رقمك ما يظهر لأحد», but the PNG/PDF/print card, which the UI recommends for the collar, prints ownerPhone (cat-id-card.tsx:275, cat-id-share.tsx:244).
  • The first notification says «تم تسجيل لولو رسمياً» (notifications.messages.ts:108; I saw it live). The Cat-ID email calls it «هوية موثّقة» and promises a Wallet pass that isn't configured.
  • The cookie banner says measurement is «مجهول» (anonymous), but lib/track.ts sends the bearer token and the API stores userId. The banner has no decline option.
Why it matters
Trust is the second emotion in the authority, and the "permission slip for money, data, health records". These are PDPL misrepresentations. The "official" wording is the regulatory risk the founder already decided to remove (AD 2.1 amendment).
Who
Every member; the company's legal exposure.
Fix
Copy fixes this week (rewrites in Trust). Rewrite the privacy policy to cover health records, clinic access, Lost & Found, transfers, processors and cross-border transfer, then have counsel review it. Offer a phone-free collar export (QR and Cat ID only) as the default. Add «بدون قياس» to the cookie banner. Add a lint or CI grep that fails on رسمي|وطني|حكومي|موثّق outside clinic contexts.
Impact
Removes the class of defect that can end a health-data company. Costs less than a day of copy work.
4
P0On a phone, the first screen has no cat and no button
Problem
On iPhone 13 (390×844) the first viewport holds: an announcement bar, a header whose loudest text is «تسجيل الدخول» (the register button is hidden below sm), a badge, a headline broken across three lines with a split marker underline, a 4-line paragraph, and a 26 px-tall name input. The «سجّل قطك» button sits directly under the cookie banner. The Cat ID card and the 3D cat start below the fold. The sticky mobile CTA (mobile-register-cta.tsx:41) only appears after cookie consent.
Why it matters
Instagram and TikTok traffic is almost all mobile. "What is it?" is answered; "what do I do next?" isn't; "why should I care?" needs the reader to finish a paragraph. The product's most persuasive object, the card, isn't seen.
Who
Mobile visitors, which is most acquisition traffic.
Fix
Mobile hero order: headline (2 lines, «هوية لقطك، لحياته كلّها»), then the live card (scaled to about 70% width, real cat photo), then the name input and button inside one 52 px pill, then the trust line. Cut the subheadline to one sentence and move the rest to the first chapter. Show the cookie notice as a slim top-of-footer strip, or delay it until first scroll, and never let it cover a CTA. Release the sticky CTA independently of consent.
Impact
Likely the single largest lever on signup rate available today.
5
P1"Member" means four things, and there's nothing to be a member of
Problem
The ceremony says «لولو صار عضو». The welcome page then shows a full price grid (199/219/329/479 ر.س) with a «الأكثر اختياراً» badge on plans nobody can buy, and says subscriptions open at 1,000 cats; nothing in code is tied to 1,000. /portal/subscribe lists «هوية مفعّلة» as a future benefit, implying today's free ID is inactive, and promises partner rates while PARTNERS = []. The account page shows a second identifier, «رقم العضوية MRC-M-…», next to the Cat ID. «عضو» is used for any cat, the first 1,000, waitlist joiners and paid subscribers.
Why it matters
R087 fixes the lexicon. The visitor's core question ("is my cat a member, and what does that get me?") gets three contradictory answers in the first two minutes. Fake social proof (R006) on a price page is the kind of detail Saudi owners screenshot.
Fix
«عضو» = has a Cat ID (free, forever). «خطة العناية» = the paid box, everywhere. Retire باقة and اشتراك. Hide the member number from owners (use the Cat ID). Remove «الأكثر اختياراً». Replace the 1,000-cat gate with «نعلن موعد فتح خطط العناية هنا وبالإيميل لمن وافق». Hide the "how benefits work" steps while there are no partners.
6
P1The main funnel never asks the questions its promises depend on
Problem
Signup after 5cda15f ("no choices, only next") issues the cat with waitlistConsent:false and sharePublicly:true (portal/cats/new/page.tsx:294,366). The welcome page promises «وأنت أول من يعرف لما نفتح», but those members are never on the waitlist. The cat goes public before the owner has seen the default or attested to the people in the photo; the disclosure arrives afterwards as a notification.
Why it matters
The 2026-08-14 amendment's guardrail #1 ("the default is disclosed at the moment it applies… the off switch right there") is broken on the path most people take. The launch-day email reaches almost nobody.
Fix
On the owner step, add one unticked line («أبغى أعرف أول ما تفتح خطط العناية») and one disclosure line with an inline switch («لولو بيظهر في مجتمع مرقط بدون اسمك — خلّه خاص»), plus the photo attestation beside the uploader. That's still effectively "only next".
7
P1Inside the product, the cat isn't the hero; a toy is
Problem
  • No photo: a photo-less cat is shown as a 300 px pink plush 3D cat on the dashboard and profile. Other cats get peach monogram tiles (ز، س), so there are two placeholder systems.
  • Card buried: the Cat ID card itself sits about 4 screens down the profile, under «الهوية والمشاركة».
  • Too long: the profile tab is about 4,300 px on mobile and re-renders summaries of every other tab (Care, Weight, Life, Safety, Share).
  • Same nudge twice: the header asks to design the card twice in a row: the «صمم بطاقة لولو» button, then the pastel «بطاقة لولو تنتظر لمستك» banner.
  • Care rows break: on mobile, titles truncate to «الفح…».
Why it matters
Principle #9. A new member's first look at their own cat's home is a stock toy and a wall of empty states.
Fix
Profile top = the Cat ID card (live, tappable to flip or share), with the add-photo action on the card. Below it, one "Now" ledger (due care, safety status). Everything else lives in its tab. Retire the 3D cat as a cat stand-in: use a single neutral silhouette frame with «أضف صورة لولو» as the empty state. Merge the two card-design prompts into one.
8
P1Signup: 8 inputs, a silent wall, and nothing survives a refresh
Problem
Name, sex, age bucket, required photo, owner name, mobile, email, then a 6-digit code: 8 inputs against the authority's north star of "under six" (R016). Next is disabled until a photo exists, with no reason given; only a red * hints at it. Step state is React-only, so a refresh or the phone's Back button loses everything, including the photo (R117). There are three time promises («خلال دقيقة» / «أقل من دقيقتين» / «دقيقتين»). The hero's «بدون بطاقة» never mentions the photo requirement. If the photo upload fails at issue time, the error is swallowed and the cat silently stays out of the community.
Fix
Make the photo skippable («أضيفها بعدين»; a faceless cat simply isn't published). Persist the draft in sessionStorage and use ?step= URLs. Show «باقي: صورة لولو» under a disabled Next. Use one time promise. Drop sex/age from signup (ask on the profile, framed as care). That gives 6 inputs.
9
P1No reason to come back, and no way to measure why
Problem
Visit #5 looks identical to visit #1 (app/portal/page.tsx): no "due this week", no "since you were here", no anniversary. A single-cat home shows a «بيتك» heading over one "Add a cat" tile. The share poster and story carry only moracat.co, with no ?ref or src. register_started, cat_id_ceremony_viewed and card_shared are defined in packages/core/src/events.ts but never sent. Reminders depend on a GitHub Actions cron that exits quietly if its secrets are missing.
Fix
The home hero shows the featured cat's next care item, unread count and «منذ آخر زيارة». Append ?ref={code}&src={poster|story|qr} to every share. Send the three funnel events. Make /admin/readiness go red when the last tick is more than 2 h old.
10
P1The design system has rules; the edges don't follow them
Problem
  • Glitter in product screens: mesh, sparkles, btn-shine and rounded-[2rem] pills appear in portal/welcome, the cat profile and community profiles. The 2026-10-02 amendment limits them to marketing.
  • Tracked Arabic: 13 Arabic strings in tracked .font-mono escape the RTL letter-spacing reset, including at the ceremony itself («نطبع هوية…»).
  • Microtype: labels on the Cat ID card are 5–8 px (cqw).
  • Lyon below display size: 217 of 328 font-display uses are below text-3xl, so Arabic loses its weight hierarchy.
  • 3D in distress contexts: 8 placements on Lost & Found.
  • Emoji: 248 remain, including «🤍 توفّى» on the bereavement action and 🐈 in the vet UI.
  • Multiple primaries: 38 files render more than one primary button.
Fix
Enforce with lint rather than review (rules in Part 05).
Part 03Top 10 opportunitiesmake it dramatically better
1
Put a real cat on the hero card

The homepage card reads «قطك» with an empty paw square. Rotate three real member cats on it (photo, name, real sequential number, «عضو مؤسِّس #681»), with owner opt-in, and let the typed name take over the card as today. A real cat with a real number is the thesis in one frame, and it's something no competitor can copy.

2
The collar tag as the first thing you can buy

The safety job («على طوقه رمز يقرأه أي جوال») depends on the owner printing a card. A metal QR tag engraved with the Cat ID makes the promise physical, gives a 49–79 ر.س first purchase that is far easier than a 199/month plan, and every tag is a walking ad that leads back to /c/[token]. It is already on the 3D commission list.

3
Approve at the counter

Consent requests today reach only an in-app notification. A clinic shows a QR; the owner, standing at the counter, scans it and taps «اسمح لعيادة … تشوف سجل لولو». It solves the delivery gap (vet P1-12), proves the consent model in person, and becomes the signature moment linking the two products.

4
Every share brings someone back with a name attached

A recipient who opens a shared story lands on «هذي هوية لولو — رقمها 681 في سجل مرقط. قطك وش رقمه؟», with the referrer credited. This turns the poster from decoration into the growth loop.

5
A home that changes every week

«هذا الأسبوع مع لولو»: next vaccine, a weigh-in nudge, an album moment one year ago, how many cats joined in your city. The care engine (W5) already computes this; the home just doesn't show it.

6
The founding number as status

The sequential serial and «عضو مؤسِّس» are the most ownable mechanics in the product. Make them visible: a certificate-grade number on the card, «دفعة الرياض 2026», and a quiet "#681 of the first 1,000" at the ceremony. This is earned status, not gamification (§04).

7
One honest clinic benefit, shown at the counter

No settlement is needed. Put «ميزة أعضاء مرقط هنا: الفحص الأول بـ 50 ر.س» in the patient header and a "benefit applied" tick on close. That gives owners the first concrete "why pay later" and gives clinics a reason to scan.

8
Lost mode as a full-screen ceremony

Reporting a lost cat is the moment of maximum emotion and maximum sharing. Make it a dedicated full-screen flow: the photo, a red ID band, poster generation, WhatsApp share and «ردود من لقوه». Then a reunion moment that closes the loop. Today these are posters plus toasts.

9
Make the yearly keepsake a calendar event

«عام لولو» exists but is buried behind Life → a year link. Surface it on the cat's anniversary and birthday, with a share card. This is the Spotify Wrapped moment the authority names (R065), and it's already built.

10
A clinic landing page

/vet redirects straight to a login form. A clinic that hears about Moracat has nowhere to understand it except the homepage's single chapter. One calm, professional page (what the clinic gets, consent tiers, the 30-second read, how to apply) turns word of mouth into applications.

The 3D language

The six commissioned objects (collar, bowl, book, heart, carrier, rosette) are a real system: one stitched-plush material, soft top-left light, three-quarter view, emerald with copper hardware. They make Moracat recognisable, and the bowl and collar on the homepage chapters work. Three things break the system:

  • The pink cat is off-palette. It's the only pink object, with a smiling face, and it's used at the largest size in the most places (hero, dashboard, profile, ceremony fallback). It's the "cutesy toy" the personality section warns about.
  • 3D is used as a stand-in for the member's own cat. An object meant for delight ends up representing a real animal, at 300 px, on every photo-less profile.
  • It breaks its own rules. 8 placements on Lost & Found ("never in distress contexts"), floating loops on the 404 and health empty states, two in view on the dashboard plus card.

Rules to adopt: emerald and copper only (the pink cat is retired, or re-rendered in the green plush); max 160 px in product UI; never as a placeholder for a real cat; never on distress, clinical or error surfaces; one per viewport; no float loops outside the homepage hero; always on a flat tinted ground, never on a gradient.

Rules to standardise (lint-enforced)

  • Radius: ban rounded-[…] outside components/home/** and the card. Allow rounded-full on controls only with aria-pressed, role=switch or square icon sizes.
  • Buttons: styled links use buttonVariants(); size="sm" only in admin and tables; one primary per view; spinners via loading.
  • Arabic type: no tracking-* on Arabic, including mono. Add an <Eyebrow> that branches by locale. Settle Lyon to text-xl and up and amend the authority. Nothing below 13 px on screen, including the card.
  • Glitter: .btn-shine, .mesh-bg-rich, <Sparkles> and the marker underline only work under a .marketing ancestor.
  • Colour: no arbitrary hsl()/hex in TSX outside document objects; no gradients in product UI; no pastel on buttons, badges or app/vet/**.
  • Bottom stack: every fixed bottom element goes through one <BottomBar> that publishes --bottom-stack; toasts and cookie notices sit above it.
  • Primitives: move Switch, Chip, Sheet, Eyebrow and DirIcon into packages/ui. QueryError becomes ErrorState. Dialogs scroll and become bottom sheets below sm.
  • Emoji: none in JSX copy; allowed only in outbound shareText.

Benchmarks, briefly

Behind: sign-in resilience and multi-device continuity (any banking or wallet app in KSA); clinic workflow reliability (ezyVet, Provet); onboarding brevity (Apple Wallet adds a card in 3 taps). Level: RTL engineering and number handling, which match the best Saudi apps (Tabby, Jahez); the consent model, which is more honest than most human health apps. Ahead: no pet product in the region (or most of the world) has a sequential, human-readable identity with a ceremony, an owner-controlled clinic record and a privacy-preserving finder page. Category-defining if finished: the Cat ID that a clinic writes to, an owner approves at the counter, a stranger can scan, and a new owner inherits. That chain is unique, and today it breaks at the clinic link.
